# Phase 3D — About Page Implementation

## Objective

Build the **About Page** for the AI & Signal Processing Hub public website.

This page must communicate the identity, purpose, vision, research orientation, technical focus, culture, and collaborative nature of the Hub.

The goal is NOT to create a generic "About Us" page.

The page should make a visitor understand:

1. Who we are
2. Why we exist
3. What we are trying to achieve
4. What areas we work in
5. How research and innovation fit into the Hub
6. How students collaborate and grow through the Hub
7. What makes the Hub relevant to Tanzania and the wider world
8. How someone can become involved

The project brief describes the AI Hub & Signal Processing Club as a student-led technical organization at Mbeya University of Science and Technology (MUST), operating within the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering / Faculty of Computing and Information Technology. The club has a strong focus on AI, Signal Processing, research, innovation, and real-world impact. Preserve this identity throughout the page.

# 15. ANIMATION

Animations should be subtle and purposeful.

Use:

- Fade-in
- Slide-up
- Staggered cards
- Text reveal
- Small technical motion
- Scroll-triggered reveals

Avoid:

- Excessive bouncing
- Random floating objects
- Constant spinning
- Overly dramatic parallax
- Animation on every element

The page should remain fast and professional.

Respect:

`prefers-reduced-motion`.
